## epx legal-entities

Reads and changes legal entities, the companies of B2B customers and their subsidiaries,
personal data included.
create and update ID (an upsert) send the JSON body of `--file`; delete ID sends none.

Its reads require the customermanagement.legalentity\_read scope.
Its writes require the customermanagement.legalentity\_manage scope.

**Command:** `epx legal-entities [flags]`, `epx legal-entities [command]`

**Flags:** only the [global flags](/commands#global-flags).

**Subcommands**

- [`epx legal-entities create`](#epx-legal-entities-create)\
  Create one legal entity.
- [`epx legal-entities delete`](#epx-legal-entities-delete)\
  Delete one legal entity.
- [`epx legal-entities get`](#epx-legal-entities-get)\
  Show one legal entity.
- [`epx legal-entities hierarchy`](#epx-legal-entities-hierarchy)\
  List a legal entity and its parents.
- [`epx legal-entities list`](#epx-legal-entities-list)\
  List legal entities.
- [`epx legal-entities search`](#epx-legal-entities-search)\
  Search legal entities.
- [`epx legal-entities update`](#epx-legal-entities-update)\
  Upsert one legal entity.

## epx legal-entities create

Create one legal entity.

Creates the legal entity that the JSON body of `--file` describes, shaped as
[`epx legal-entities get ID -o json`](/commands/legal-entities#epx-legal-entities-get) prints one:
name (required), type (COMPANY, the default, or SUBSIDIARY), parentId (the legal
entity a subsidiary belongs to), legalInfo, accountLimit, customerGroups (IAM groups),
entitiesAddresses and approvalGroup (its customer locations and contact assignments),
restrictions and mixins; parentId, customerGroups, entitiesAddresses and approvalGroup must
name items that exist (400 Bad Request, exit 1, otherwise), the last three each as
`{"id": ...}` or in full, as get prints it.
Without an id, Emporix makes one, and answers with it; an id that exists already is a
conflict (exit 5).
A create that Emporix refuses as a conflict may still make the IAM customer groups of
the legal entity.

Changes data: on a terminal, epx asks before it sends POST. `--yes` sends without
asking (required without a terminal, or with `--file` -); `--dry-run` prints the request
instead of sending it.

Requires the customermanagement.legalentity\_manage scope.

**Command:** `epx legal-entities create [flags]`

**Flags**

- `--dry-run`\
  Print the request instead of sending it (excludes `-o`).
- `--file <PATH>`\
  JSON request body in PATH, sent byte for byte (- reads standard input; sending it then needs `--yes`; required).
- `-o, --output <format>`\
  Format of the response: json or yaml. Default: `json`.
- `--yes`\
  Send without asking (required without a terminal, or with `--file` -).

Also takes the [global flags](/commands#global-flags).

## epx legal-entities delete

Delete one legal entity.

Deletes the legal entity ID; it sends no body.
ID is the ID that [`epx legal-entities list`](/commands/legal-entities#epx-legal-entities-list) shows; an unknown one is answered as one that Emporix deleted (204 No Content).
The assignments are the contact assignments of it and of its subsidiaries and the segment
assignments that name it; the IAM customer groups those still in its customerGroups. Its
customer locations stay, and its customers no longer name it (one with no other legal
entity becomes a B2C customer).

Deletes the legal entity with its subsidiaries, assignments and IAM customer groups with one request, which cannot be undone.
On a terminal, epx sends DELETE only once you type ID at its prompt.
`--yes` sends without asking (required without a terminal);
`--dry-run` prints the request instead of sending it.

Requires the customermanagement.legalentity\_manage scope.

**Command:** `epx legal-entities delete ID [flags]`

**Flags**

- `--dry-run`\
  Print the request instead of sending it (excludes `-o`).
- `-o, --output <format>`\
  Format of the response: json or yaml. Default: `json`.
- `--yes`\
  Send without asking (required without a terminal).

Also takes the [global flags](/commands#global-flags).

## epx legal-entities get

Show one legal entity.

Reads legal entities, the companies of B2B customers and their subsidiaries, personal data
included (approval groups name customers). TYPE is COMPANY or SUBSIDIARY, PARENT the legal
entity a subsidiary belongs to, COUNTRY the country of registration. Registration details,
addresses, customer groups, approval groups and restrictions are only in `-o json`.

Requires the customermanagement.legalentity\_read scope.

**Command:** `epx legal-entities get ID [flags]`

**Flags**

- `--columns <names>`\
  Print only these columns of table and csv, in this order: comma-separated names (any case) among ID, NAME, TYPE, PARENT, LEGAL NAME, COUNTRY, ACCOUNT LIMIT.
- `--no-headers`\
  Print table and csv without the header row.
- `-o, --output <format>`\
  Output format: table, json, yaml or csv (csv: the table's rows; json and yaml: the whole response). Default: `table`.

Also takes the [global flags](/commands#global-flags).

## epx legal-entities hierarchy

List a legal entity and its parents.

Reads a legal entity and every one above it, up to its company, in the order Emporix
sends them, personal data included; the columns are those of [`epx legal-entities list`](/commands/legal-entities#epx-legal-entities-list).
LEGAL\_ENTITY\_ID is the ID that [`epx legal-entities list`](/commands/legal-entities#epx-legal-entities-list) shows; an unknown one lists nothing.

Requires the customermanagement.legalentity\_read scope.

**Command:** `epx legal-entities hierarchy LEGAL_ENTITY_ID [flags]`

**Flags**

- `--columns <names>`\
  Print only these columns of table and csv, in this order: comma-separated names (any case) among ID, NAME, TYPE, PARENT, LEGAL NAME, COUNTRY, ACCOUNT LIMIT.
- `--limit <n>`\
  Print at most n items; paged reads stop early where they safely can (0: every item).
- `--no-headers`\
  Print table and csv without the header row.
- `-o, --output <format>`\
  Output format: table, json, yaml or csv (csv: the table's rows; json and yaml: the whole response). Default: `table`.
- `--stream`\
  Print items as JSON Lines as soon as they are checked, page by page on paged lists, reading up to 10000 pages where a list without it stops after 1000: a check failing on a later page exits 1 after the pages before it (excludes `-o`, `--columns` and `--no-headers`).

Also takes the [global flags](/commands#global-flags).

without it, every legal entity).

flags make or `--body` gives whole.

## epx legal-entities update

Upsert one legal entity.

Replaces the legal entity ID with the JSON body of `--file`, shaped as create takes it
but without an id, or creates it with that id where none exists (an upsert); it requires
name and type.
With metadata.version as [`epx legal-entities get ID -o json`](/commands/legal-entities#epx-legal-entities-get) prints it, Emporix
replaces it only if nobody changed it since, and answers 409 Conflict (exit 5) otherwise;
without metadata.version, it replaces it whatever its version.
ID is the ID that [`epx legal-entities list`](/commands/legal-entities#epx-legal-entities-list) shows.
Keep every customer group that Emporix made for the legal entity (Admin, Buyer, Requester
and Contact) in customerGroups: without the Contact group, Emporix deletes that group from
IAM; without another, it leaves that group in IAM, where deleting the legal entity no
longer removes it.

Changes data: on a terminal, epx asks before it sends PUT. `--yes` sends without
asking (required without a terminal, or with `--file` -); `--dry-run` prints the request
instead of sending it.

Requires the customermanagement.legalentity\_manage scope.

**Command:** `epx legal-entities update ID [flags]`

**Flags**

- `--dry-run`\
  Print the request instead of sending it (excludes `-o`).
- `--file <PATH>`\
  JSON request body in PATH, sent byte for byte (- reads standard input; sending it then needs `--yes`; required).
- `-o, --output <format>`\
  Format of the response: json or yaml. Default: `json`.
- `--yes`\
  Send without asking (required without a terminal, or with `--file` -).

Also takes the [global flags](/commands#global-flags).
